About the Event

Qiskit Fall Fest is a series of quantum computing events organised by advocates of Qiskit from around the world, supported by IBM Quantum. The event being held at Deakin University, brings together learners and researchers for a good time of collaboration and discovery.

What to Expect

  • Introductory sessions on quantum computing
  • Qiskit Fundamentals
  • Networking session
