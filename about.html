About the Event

Qiskit Fall Fest is a series of quantum computing events organised by students around the world, supported by IBM Quantum. The Deakin University edition brings together learners and researchers for a week of collaboration and discovery.

What to Expect

  • Introductory sessions on quantum computing and Qiskit
  • Hands-on coding workshops
  • A quantum hackathon with prizes
  • Networking with industry and academia
